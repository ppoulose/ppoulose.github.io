Preparation of Dilute HCl

Calculate the serial dilution steps required to prepare HCl at a specified target pH.

Principle

For dilute HCl, assuming complete dissociation:

pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]

Dilution is calculated using:

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

Laboratory safety

Wear safety goggles, gloves and a lab coat. Always add acid to water, never water to acid. Work under teacher supervision.

Use calibrated volumetric glassware. Check the pH experimentally after each dilution.
