Preparation of pH 4 HCl

Neutralization Calculator Prepare pH 4 HCl

Calculate the volume of stock hydrochloric acid required to prepare a dilute HCl solution at pH 4.

Principle

For dilute HCl, assuming complete dissociation:

[H+] = 10^-pH

The dilution calculation uses:

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

The target concentration for pH 4 is 0.0001 mol/L.

Laboratory safety

Wear appropriate eye protection, gloves and a lab coat. Add acid slowly to water, never water to concentrated acid. Prepare solutions under teacher supervision.

For very small calculated volumes, use a suitable calibrated micropipette or prepare an intermediate dilution.
